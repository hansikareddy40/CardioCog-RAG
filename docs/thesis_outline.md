## Writing rules that keep plagiarism near zero

1. **Use the reference theses for layout only.** Do not copy or lightly reword any sentence from them. Your topic is different, so there is no reason to.
2. **Every number comes from our own results**, with its source in `docs/claims_verification.md`.
3. **Every idea from a paper gets a citation**, even when written in your own words.
4. **Write explanations in your own words after you understand them.** The study guide and the notebooks are there so you can explain each section without reading it out.
5. **Quote sparingly**, in quotation marks, with a reference: for example the NACC acknowledgement, which must be copied word for word.
6. **Declare AI assistance if your department's rules require it.** Hidden AI use is the risk; declared use usually is not.
